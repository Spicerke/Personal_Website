Claim Detection

nlp · serving
try it live ↗ · github.com/Spicerke/Claim_Detection ↗

A take-home from a technical interview: decide whether a given sentence is a factual claim.

Data

I fine-tuned DistilBERT — small enough to train locally — on a mix of two datasets. ClaimBuster (political debate sentences) skews heavily toward non-claims, so I balanced it with FEVER's 185k Wikipedia-derived claims until the label distribution was even. Around 90% accuracy on a held-out set.

Serving

A model isn't a product, so I wrapped it: a FastAPI service exposing one endpoint, guarded by input-length validation and a rate limiter, with a plain HTML/CSS/JS frontend and the whole thing Dockerized.

Load testing

Then I load-tested it with Locust to see where it broke. It held a high request volume at a low error rate with roughly 200ms average response time.
